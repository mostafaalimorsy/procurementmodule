How is delivery evidence calculated?
  • Variances = (actual − awarded) ÷ awarded, from finalized closeouts only. A project without an awarded value or duration is left out of that figure, never counted as late or over budget.
  • Quality and HSE are your company's internal 1–5 ratings, not a certification. They are never combined into one score here.
  • The composite is the documented outcome-score rule a recommendation uses when it weighs past performance: the whole-number mean of each project's score (quality, HSE, cost, schedule, would-work-again). It is shown with its sample because one project is not a trend.
