How are these figures calculated?
  • Response reliability = bids submitted ÷ valid invitations. An invitation is valid once its bidding has closed, unless it was withdrawn or the tender was cancelled before closing. Invitations still open are shown apart.
  • Time to first bid = from the invitation being issued to the firm's first submitted bid; time to first reply also counts an intention to bid or a decline.
  • Award rate = awards ÷ awarded tenders in which the firm's bid was opened. Tenders still being decided, or cancelled after opening, are not counted as losses. A reserve is not an award.
  • Price position = the firm's leveled price against the median leveled price of the same tender's bids in the same currency, as a signed percentage. The median includes this firm's own bid, so in a tender with two bids the two firms sit at equal and opposite positions. No competitor's price is shown and no currency is converted.
  • Evidence strength counts finalized closeouts: limited 1–2, moderate 3–5, strong 6 or more. It describes how much evidence exists, not a prediction.
